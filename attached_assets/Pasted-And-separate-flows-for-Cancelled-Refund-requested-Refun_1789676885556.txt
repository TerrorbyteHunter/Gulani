Cancelled
Refund requested

4. Customer Management

Vendors should be able to see customers who have purchased from them.

For example:

Customer

Name
Number/email where appropriate
Orders
Total spent
Last purchase
Current orders
Reviews

But I'd avoid exposing unnecessary customer information. The platform should control what personal data vendors can access.

5. Messaging

This is particularly important for your Gulani concept.

You previously wanted:

Customer ↔ Customer Service/Admin

and

Vendor ↔ Customer Service/Admin

I'd also consider:

Customer ↔ Vendor

but with platform moderation and protections.

Features:

Text messages
Order-linked conversations
Image attachments
Automated order messages
Read/unread status
Notifications
Report conversation
Block/restrict user
Admin intervention

For example:

Customer: "Is this phone available in black?"

Vendor can respond without the customer having to leave Gulani.

6. Inventory

I'd make inventory its own section rather than burying everything under Products.

Features:

Current stock
Low-stock threshold
Out-of-stock products
Stock adjustments
Stock history
Incoming stock
Reserved stock
Sold stock
SKU management

For example:

iPhone 15 Pro

Available: 12
Reserved: 2
Sold today: 3
Low-stock threshold: 5

This becomes very useful once you have many vendors.

Wallet

Sales                 K10,000
